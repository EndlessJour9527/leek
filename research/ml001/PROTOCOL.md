## Features, target and time isolation
16 fixed causal features: total-return momentum 1/5/10/20/60 sessions; one-day total-return volatility 5/20/60; total-return level versus trailing means 5/20/60; current volume versus trailing volume means 5/20; intraday (high-low)/close; close/open-1; ex-dividend-neutralized opening gap (open+cash_ex)/previous_close-1. Rolling windows include the observed close, never future observations. Features start after 60-session history. Zero variance is supported; nonfinite features are blocked, not silently filled from future data.

Target at close t: economic gross per-unit return from raw open(t+1) to raw open(t+6), plus cash dividends with ex dates strictly after entry and at/before exit, divided by entry price. Require record date immediately precedes ex date in the session calendar; otherwise block this label convention. This is NOT the close(t) to close(t+5) return. Labels become usable only after their endpoint. Live historical publication times are assumed rather than certified.

Four expanding yearly folds, classified by next-session execution year 2022–2025. For each fold: training/validation labels must finish BEFORE the first forecast decision session; latest 126 eligible decision samples are validation; fitting samples precede validation with their label endpoints strictly before validation's first decision. At least 350 fitting samples. All preprocessing is fitted only on fit rows. Annual refitting; no random train/test shuffle; no validation/test lookahead. Model fits and their split boundaries are retained. Predictions are saved before the cost/account evaluation. Later folds may legitimately train on matured earlier-fold observations, never rewrite their prior predictions.

## Fixed learned candidates and controls
- Ridge: StandardScaler fitted on fitting rows only, alpha=1.0, solver=svd. No alpha search.
- LightGBM: CPU, one thread, regression/L2 objective, max 200 boosting rounds, learning_rate=0.03, num_leaves=7, max_depth=3, min_data_in_leaf=60, lambda_l2=1.0, deterministic=true, force_col_wise=true, seed=20261025. Early stopping after 20 rounds without validation-L2 improvement; test targets never passed to fit. No refit using validation or parameter search after scores.
- Controls: zero forecast and training-mean forecast for predictive metrics; zero-interest cash, same-budget buy-and-hold, weekly MA120 for account metrics. Existing daily MA120/H001/H002/H003 are not rewritten.

Portfolio policy is separate from prediction: decide every 5 observed sessions beginning at the last close before the evaluation start; learned target ON iff predicted gross 5-session return >0.0025, otherwise OFF; maintain state between decisions. MA120 weekly uses close-TR above inclusive MA120 and same decision dates, equality retains state. Long-only, no leverage, same existing initial research cash 50,000 / approximate 20% entry budget / 100-share lot / adverse 0.001 tick rounding / original base and stress fees / record-ex-pay accounting. Reuse existing Ledger and continuous accounting; no forced terminal liquidation. Entry quantity is set at prior close and can overshoot reference fraction on an opening gap: known limitation retained equally, not a new hard risk limit. No additional paid service or real orders.

## Deliverable and decision
Actual trained parameter files per yearly fold; model reload consistency; dated forecasts and fit/validation/maturity boundaries; predictive MSE vs zero and train-mean, time-series rank association (NOT cross-sectional IC); base/stress continuous NAV and trades, exposure, costs, dividends, annual results, benchmark differences and rejection/terminal positions. Correlated overlapping labels and small completed-trade counts must be explicit. Report both candidates regardless of outcome.

Diagnostic screen fixed before results: learned model must beat both predictive baselines by MSE AND beat weekly MA120 and same-budget buy-hold by net total account return in both cost cases, without a deeper close drawdown than the passive baseline in either case, and have at least 20 closed trades. Passing is at most NEEDS_INDEPENDENT_VALIDATION; failing any requirement is REJECTED_FOR_PROMOTION. These are pilot screening criteria, not statistical significance, risk-adjusted alpha certification or the original M2/M3 gates. No automatic promotion in either case.
